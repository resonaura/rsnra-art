The programs under public/legacy/programs are vendored, with minimal
changes, from the 98.js project (https://github.com/1j01/98), which
brings together several independently authored Windows 98 clones.

Calculator
  Windows 98 interface by Isaiah Odhner (https://github.com/1j01/98)
  Web port of the Windows 10 Calculator engine ("mcalculator") by
  Muzam1l (https://github.com/Muzammil-Xhah/mcalculator) — MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2020 muzamil

Sound Recorder
  by Isaiah Odhner (https://github.com/1j01/98)

Minesweeper (face/tile icon sprite only, see public/icons/minesweeper-*;
game logic is our own)
  by Jon Ziebell (https://github.com/ziebelje/minesweeper)

Solitaire
  by Radovan Janjic (https://github.com/rjanjic/js-solitaire) —
  MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 Radovan Janjic

3D Pinball for Windows - Space Cadet
  decompiled & ported to WebAssembly by Muzychenko Andrey and Alula
  (https://github.com/alula/SpaceCadetPinball)

Shared runtime (public/legacy/lib, public/legacy/src)
  os-gui window/menu toolkit and msgbox by Isaiah Odhner
  (https://github.com/1j01/os-gui)

Full MIT license texts for the above are available in their
respective upstream repositories.
